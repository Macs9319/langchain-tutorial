# Servicing — Pedalwise Bikes

## Free first service
Every new bike includes a free safety check and adjustment within the first
60 days. Cables stretch as a new bike beds in, so we strongly recommend it.

## Service packages
- Basic tune-up: 45 EUR. Gear and brake adjustment, tyre check, safety check.
- Full service: 120 EUR. Everything in the basic tune-up plus a strip-down,
  deep clean, bearing check and drivetrain replacement if worn (parts extra).
- E-bike service: 150 EUR. Full service plus motor diagnostics and a firmware
  update.

## Booking
Services are booked online and usually take 2 working days. Same-day
"express" service is available Monday to Thursday for a 25 EUR surcharge.

## Opening hours
Workshops are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00. Closed on Sundays and
public holidays.
