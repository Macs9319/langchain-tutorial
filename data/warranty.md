# Warranty — Pedalwise Bikes

## Frames
All Pedalwise frames carry a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects
for the original owner. Crash damage is not covered, but owners get 40% off a
replacement frame through the crash replacement programme.

## Components
Components (gears, brakes, wheels) are covered for 2 years. Normal wear items
such as tyres, brake pads, chains and cables are not covered.

## Electric bikes
E-bike motors are covered for 3 years. Batteries are covered for 2 years or
500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. A battery is considered faulty
if it holds less than 70% of its original capacity within that period.

## Making a claim
Email warranty@pedalwise.example with your order number and photos of the
problem. Claims are usually answered within 3 business days.
