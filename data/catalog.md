# Product Catalog — Pedalwise Bikes

All products and part numbers are fictional.

## Bikes

### Trail 500 mountain bike
Full-suspension mountain bike with 140 mm of travel, hydraulic disc brakes and
a 12-speed drivetrain. Frame sizes S, M, L. Price: 899 EUR.

### Gravel 300
Drop-bar gravel bike for mixed roads and trails, with clearance for 45 mm
tyres and mounts for racks and bottles. Frame sizes 50, 54, 58 cm. Price: 1099 EUR.

### City e-bike
Step-through electric bike with a 250 W hub motor, a 500 Wh battery (range
about 80 km) and built-in lights. One size fits riders 155-190 cm. Price: 2199 EUR.

### Kids 20
20-inch wheel bike for children aged 6-9, with a lightweight aluminium frame
and two hand brakes. Price: 279 EUR.

## Spare parts

### PW-BRK-220 brake pads
Organic disc brake pads for the Trail 500 and the Gravel 300. Quieter than
metal pads, but wear faster in wet weather. Price: 18 EUR per pair.

### PW-BRK-230 brake pads
Sintered metal disc brake pads for the Trail 500 and the City e-bike. Last
longer in mud and rain. Price: 22 EUR per pair.

### PW-BAT-500 battery
Spare 500 Wh battery for the City e-bike. Charges fully in about 5 hours.
Price: 649 EUR.

### PW-CHN-12 chain
12-speed chain for the Trail 500. Replace it every 2000-3000 km. Price: 35 EUR.

### PW-TYR-45 tyre
45 mm gravel tyre for the Gravel 300, tubeless-ready. Price: 42 EUR.

## Compatibility notes

The City e-bike uses a different brake mount from the Gravel 300, so
PW-BRK-220 pads do not fit it. The Kids 20 uses rim brakes, so none of the
disc brake pads fit it.
