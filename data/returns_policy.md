# Returns and Refunds Policy — Pedalwise Bikes

Pedalwise Bikes is a fictional bicycle shop used in these tutorials.

## Return window
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Bicycles that have been ridden can be returned within 14 days, but a 10%
restocking fee applies. Custom-built bikes cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
Log in to your account, open the order, and choose "Start a return". You will
receive a prepaid shipping label by email within one business day. Pack the
item in its original box where possible.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of
the item arriving at our warehouse. Store credit is issued instantly and
includes a 5% bonus.
